Compost Calculator: plan a pile with the materials you have

Calculate compost pile volume, ingredient proportions and an estimated carbon-to-nitrogen ratio (C:N) with BioFarmLab’s Living Soil Shortcut app.

BioFarmLab·10/11/2026·updated 11/11/2026

Compost Calculator is a tool in BioFarmLab’s Living Soil Shortcut app for calculating compost pile volume, ingredient proportions and an estimated carbon-to-nitrogen ratio (C:N). You plan a mix with the materials you have, then save the named pile to Compost Log to follow the process.

How can I calculate a compost mix?

Enter the planned pile dimensions and the materials you want to use. The calculator brings three results together:

  • Pile volume: the amount of space your planned pile will occupy.
  • Ingredient proportions: how much each material contributes to the mix.
  • Estimated C:N: the calculated balance of carbon and nitrogen in the planned mixture.

You can review those results while planning the pile, with the calculations in the same tool as the recipe. If you already make compost, this puts calculations you would otherwise work out separately into one place. The course provides the knowledge behind choosing and using the materials.

From a planned pile to a record you can return to

The calculator starts with planned pile dimensions. You give the pile a name and a start date, build its recipe, then save it to Compost Log. That name makes it possible to return to the same pile as the process develops.

For example, a pile prepared from autumn garden materials has its own starting plan. Later temperature and moisture records belong to that pile rather than to an anonymous list of measurements. You can look back at what you planned alongside the history of what you did.

A calculator and the knowledge behind the recipe

Living Soil Shortcut connects the tool with lessons on compost ingredients, small- and larger-scale composting, troubleshooting and application. The course explains the jobs that materials perform in a pile. The calculator supports planning with your own materials.

The three tools have connected roles: Compost Calculator helps plan the pile, Compost Log keeps its process history, and Soil Radar records observations of the ground you are working to improve.

Where to get Compost Calculator

The calculator comes with the Living Soil Shortcut course and app package. The app is available for iPhone, iPad and Android; the English-language course contains 28 short lessons with lifetime course access.
